The story

Itakao is the son of Lord Ikagawa, ruler and head of the Kurosaka clan. He spends his days away from the fortress, helping the people who live around it, until a retainer runs to find him with news: his father has been killed by a band of ronin.

Now lord himself, Itakao takes the road to find the men who did it. Each duel ends with a few last words, and some of them leave him clues about who sent the ronin.

How a duel works

A sword covers its own zone, so the rule is simple: hit where their sword isn't.

  • The crank is your sword arm: up guards high, forward guards the middle, down guards low.
  • Ⓑ parries. Watch your opponent wind up, put your sword in the zone he is attacking and press Ⓑ just before the blow lands. Hold the right guard without pressing anything and you simply block.
  • Ⓐ strikes. After a parry his guard is knocked aside for a moment: strike where it is open.
  • A perfect parry, at the very last instant, slows time down. Strike then and you dash straight through him.
  • Both of you can take three wounds. Swinging wildly gets read and punished.

What's in it

  • Six opponents, each with their own fighting style, stage and story
  • Story scenes between the duels, told in short dialogue and memories
  • A tutorial for the basics, offered the first time you play
  • Duels mode (⬇️ on the title screen) to fight any of the six opponents without the story
  • Three difficulties, a left- or right-handed crank stance and crank sensitivity, all in the Playdate's menu
  • An original score and sound effects

How to install it

  1. Download CRANKBLADE.pdx.zip above.
  2. Sideload it. Sign in at play.date/account/sideload and upload the zip; it arrives on your Playdate the next time it checks for games (Settings, Games). Or reboot the Playdate to Data Disk (Settings, System), unzip, copy CRANKBLADE.pdx into the Games folder and eject.
  3. Play. Ⓐ on the title starts the story; ⬇️ opens Duels mode.

It is too hard

Open the Playdate's menu during a duel and set difficulty to easy: your opponents give you more time to read them and longer parry windows. The tutorial on the title screen (Ⓑ) teaches the basics again.

I hold the crank the other way

Set stance to left in the Playdate's menu. The sword then points forward when the crank handle points toward you.
