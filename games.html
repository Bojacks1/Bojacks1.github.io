Games

Made for a crank.

The Playdate is a pocket-sized console with a handle on its side. These are the games and tools I made for it, written in Lua and C.

CRANKBLADE →

Playdate · Lua

A one-on-one samurai duel. The crank is your sword: turn it to guard high, middle or low, then parry or strike with the buttons.

Six opponents, each harder than the last, a perfect-parry slow motion, an original score, and a story about a lord hunting his father's killers.

  • Crank aims your sword: up, forward or down
  • B parries  ·  A strikes

Cam Viewfinder →

Free tool · Playdate + Mac

Turns your Playdate into a live camera screen. A small Mac app, Playdate Cam Link, sends your camera over the USB cable, and the Playdate shows it in pure black and white.

Pick any camera on your Mac, in full or half size. The picture here is an example of the look, made from a still photo.

  • Mac app picks the camera and the size
  • USB cable carries the picture to the Playdate
Free download  ·  On itch.io

Crank Tunes →

Free tool · Playdate + Mac or Linux

Your own music on your Playdate. A small server on your computer streams the songs in a folder over Wi-Fi, and the Playdate shows each song's cover art in 1-bit while it plays.

It is also a remote for Spotify or another music app on the computer: see the song and its cover, pause, skip and turn the volume with the crank.

  • Crank scrolls the list and turns the volume
  • A plays  ·  B big cover in the remote
Free download On itch.io

Crank Chat →

Experiment · Playdate + Mac or Linux

Can you text from a Playdate? I built this to find out, and yes, you can. Your conversations show as chat bubbles, and you type with the crank keyboard or send a quick reply.

A small relay on your computer passes the messages to a Telegram bot you make yourself or, on a Mac, to real phone texts through the Messages app.

  • Crank scrolls and picks each letter
  • A opens and types  ·  B quick replies
Free download  ·  On itch.io

Crank FM

Playdate · C

Internet radio, tuned with the crank. Turn the dial through the static and a station crackles into focus, with the song title on a little radio-shaped screen.

Six bands of six stations, each shown as a key on the front. Streams come from SomaFM.

  • Crank turns the dial
  • Left / Right jump to a station  ·  B changes band

Vinyl

Playdate · Lua

A turntable in your hands. Flip through a crate of records, drop the needle, and use the crank as your hand on the platter: push it, drag it, scratch it.

The platter has real momentum, so the music speeds up, slows down and runs backwards as you turn.

  • Crank nudges and scratches the record
  • A starts and stops the motor  ·  B back to the crate

Mr Cook

Playdate · Lua

A calm cooking game with no score, no timer and no way to fail. The crank is the knife and the kettle; tilting the console slides things around the board.

The first recipe is a bowl of ham ramen, from boiling the water to the last topping.

  • Crank chops, pours and stirs
  • Tilt moves things and sets the size of each cut
