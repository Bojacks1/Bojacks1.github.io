Games

Made for a crank.

The Playdate is a pocket-sized console with a handle on its side. These are four games I made for it, written in Lua and C. Browser versions are in the works, so you will be able to try them right here.

CRANKBLADE

Playdate · Lua

A one-on-one samurai duel. The crank is your sword: turn it to guard high, middle or low, then parry or strike with the buttons.

Six opponents, each harder than the last, a perfect-parry slow motion, and a story about a lord hunting his father's killers.

  • Crank aims your sword: up, forward or down
  • B parries  ·  A strikes

Crank FM

Playdate · C

Internet radio, tuned with the crank. Turn the dial through the static and a station crackles into focus, with the song title on a little radio-shaped screen.

Six bands of six stations, each shown as a key on the front. Streams come from SomaFM.

  • Crank turns the dial
  • Left / Right jump to a station  ·  B changes band

Vinyl

Playdate · Lua

A turntable in your hands. Flip through a crate of records, drop the needle, and use the crank as your hand on the platter: push it, drag it, scratch it.

The platter has real momentum, so the music speeds up, slows down and runs backwards as you turn.

  • Crank nudges and scratches the record
  • A starts and stops the motor  ·  B back to the crate

Mr Cook

Playdate · Lua

A calm cooking game with no score, no timer and no way to fail. The crank is the knife and the kettle; tilting the console slides things around the board.

The first recipe is a bowl of ham ramen, from boiling the water to the last topping.

  • Crank chops, pours and stirs
  • Tilt moves things and sets the size of each cut
